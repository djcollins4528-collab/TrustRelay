ACCESSIBILITY

TrustRelay accessibility commitment

TrustRelay is intended to be usable by people with a wide range of abilities, devices, and assistive technologies. Accessibility is treated as a product-quality requirement rather than a marketing certification.

No conformance claim yet. TrustRelay is not currently representing the service as independently audited or formally certified against WCAG, Section 508, EN 301 549, or another accessibility standard. The target for ongoing product work is WCAG 2.2 Level AA where applicable.

Current approach

Built to support accessible use.

Product and public-site work is reviewed for common accessibility barriers as the platform evolves.

Keyboard use

Interactive controls are designed to remain reachable and operable without requiring a pointing device.

Semantic structure

Pages use headings, labels, landmarks, native controls, and descriptive text so assistive technologies can interpret structure and purpose.

Readable presentation

Layouts are responsive and designed to preserve usable hierarchy, spacing, contrast, and text readability across screen sizes.

Clear error handling

Forms and workflows aim to communicate validation and error states in text rather than relying on color alone.

Feedback

Report an accessibility barrier.

If a TrustRelay workflow, document, or public page is difficult to use with assistive technology, report the specific barrier so it can be reproduced and prioritized.

Send accessibility feedback Open Support

Helpful report details

Include the page or workflow, what you were trying to accomplish, the browser or device, assistive technology if applicable, and what prevented completion. Do not include passwords, authentication codes, private keys, or unnecessary personal information.
