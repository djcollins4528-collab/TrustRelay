# TrustRelay Founding Design Partner Conversion Playbook

Updated: 2026-10-03 UTC

Purpose: convert a design-partner conversation into measurable pilot evidence and, after TrustRelay's commercial launch gates are complete, a paying customer.

## Qualification

A pilot is qualified when the prospect can identify:
1. one recurring workflow where a person acts for another person, estate, trust, or dependent;
2. an operational owner for that workflow;
3. approximate monthly authority-check volume;
4. current handling time or escalation burden;
5. at least one measurable failure/friction mode such as repeated documents, manual calls, ambiguous scope, stale authority, or missing audit evidence.

Prefer prospects with 25+ authority checks/month or high-cost/high-risk cases even at lower volume.

## First call

The first call is 20 minutes:
- 3 min: prospect describes the current authority-verification workflow;
- 5 min: quantify volume, handling time, escalations and common failure modes;
- 7 min: demonstrate grant -> acceptance -> verifier decision -> revocation;
- 3 min: choose one pilot workflow and internal owner;
- 2 min: agree on next step/date.

Do not turn the first call into a broad product tour.

## Pilot offer

- 60 days
- no cost
- one narrowly defined workflow
- portal first
- Partner API only after value is demonstrated
- founder-led implementation
- weekly 20-minute working session
- baseline vs pilot outcome report

Pilot success metrics:
- median authority-decision time
- repeated-document requests per case
- manual touches per case
- escalations per 100 cases
- ambiguous/over-broad permissions identified
- revocation propagation time
- audit completeness

## Commercial conversion

At or before day 30, if the pilot is showing value, ask the prospect to confirm conditional commercial intent in writing, subject to successful pilot completion, procurement/security review, TrustRelay commercial launch readiness, and mutually acceptable final terms.

For smaller organizations whose use fits the standard Growth plan, use the existing $299/month Growth plan as the initial commercial anchor.

For enterprise, embedded, API, white-label, or materially higher-volume deployments, do not anchor at $299/month. Gather expected volumes, integration requirements, support requirements and procurement/security obligations, then price separately.

Do not take payment until TrustRelay's required commercial launch controls are green.

## Reply handling

Positive reply:
"Great — the fastest way to determine fit is to pick one authority workflow and put numbers around it. Could we use 20 minutes to map (1) who presents the authority, (2) who verifies it, (3) roughly how many cases you see, and (4) where the current process slows down? I’ll show the working TrustRelay flow against that exact case."

"Send information":
Send https://trustrelay-production.onrender.com/pilot/ and ask for one specific workflow plus approximate monthly volume. Avoid sending a generic deck unless requested.

"Not a priority":
Ask one question only: "Understood. Before I close this out, is delegated-authority verification handled cleanly in your existing system, or is the issue simply not important enough right now?" Use the answer as market research.

"Security/compliance concern":
Point to the Security Statement and explain that paid production remains fail-closed pending the final legal/domain/retention gates. Never overstate certification or legal readiness.

"Too early":
Offer a 20-minute workflow interview without asking them to run a pilot.

## Follow-up cadence

- Day 0: personalized first touch.
- Day 4-5: concise follow-up with one concrete use case.
- Day 10-12: send a sharper outcome hypothesis relevant to their business.
- Day 20-25: final close-the-loop note.
- Stop after three follow-ups unless the prospect engages.

Never send repeated same-day touches to the same prospect.

## Pipeline priority

A: direct authority pain + integration/distribution potential.
B: direct pain but slower/less strategic route.
C: weak fit, inactive route, or superseded company.

The immediate revenue goal is:
- 3 qualified pilots
- at least 1 written conditional commercial-intent confirmation
- first paid customer immediately after commercial launch controls are complete
- 5 paying organizations as the first product-market-fit checkpoint.
