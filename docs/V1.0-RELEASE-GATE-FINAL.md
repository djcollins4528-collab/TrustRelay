## Remaining human/provider gates

Commercial launch now requires decisions or actions outside autonomous staging engineering:

1. approve/provision the separate production Supabase project and any resulting paid plan/cost;
2. provide/control the production custom domain/DNS and transactional SMTP;
3. select the TrustRelay Stripe account, commercial pricing, live price IDs and webhook configuration;
4. confirm the contracting legal entity and obtain counsel approval of the binding legal package and retention periods/actions;
5. designate monitored security/privacy/incident contacts and at least two independently approved, trained identity reviewers;
6. select/configure a dedicated malware-scanning/quarantine provider for evidence uploads;
7. confirm production Auth abuse/CAPTCHA, database network restrictions, SSL posture, and backup/restore evidence.

Until those gates are satisfied, TrustRelay correctly remains a **technical production candidate**, not a commercially activated production service.
