# TrustRelay v1.0 Production Readiness

Date: 2026-10-01

## Release posture

TrustRelay v1.0 is a **technical production candidate**. Commercial production activation is intentionally fail-closed.

The application, database, Partner API, identity/evidence system, webhooks, notification system, RBAC, audit exports, onboarding engine, billing adapter and legal versioning are implemented. A production organization cannot enter live mode until both organization-specific and platform-wide gates pass.

## Runtime and webhook egress

The hardened Node staging runtime is deployed at:

`https://trustrelay-v1-runtime-staging.onrender.com`

It serves the v1.0 candidate with CSP/HSTS/security headers and an internal webhook egress relay.

Production webhook delivery now uses a pinned-DNS TLS relay:

- the relay resolves the destination hostname itself;
- all resolved addresses must be public;
- the actual TLS socket connects to the validated IP;
- SNI and certificate validation remain bound to the original hostname;
- redirects are not followed;
- destination ports other than 443 are rejected;
- the relay endpoint requires a Vault-backed internal bearer secret.

Real staging verification passed:

- public HTTPS relay probe: HTTP 200;
- private literal target `127.0.0.1`: HTTP 403 `WEBHOOK_PRIVATE_TARGET`;
- queue -> dispatcher -> relay -> receiver: delivered in one attempt;
- persisted payload SHA-256 matched the receiver's body SHA-256;
- timestamp matched;
- HMAC-SHA256 recomputation matched;
- target User-Agent remained `TrustRelay-Webhook/0.9`.

The disposable receiver and test records were retired/removed after verification.

## Backup posture

The connected Supabase organization is on the Free plan, and TrustRelay has no separately validated off-site backup/restore evidence configured for production. Therefore `backup_recovery` is explicitly **fail**, not unknown.

The disaster-recovery runbook exists, but a production backup configuration and tested restore exercise are still required.

## Legal

The Legal & Trust Center contains v1.0 draft documents with SHA-256 hashes.

Canonical legal records are published for review but are:

- `binding=false`
- `counsel_reviewed=false`
- `effective_at=null`

Therefore they do not trigger consumer or organization legal acceptance.

The service-role publication function refuses to make a document effective/binding unless counsel review and binding status are explicitly confirmed.

## Organization activation gates

An organization must have:

- HTTPS website
- primary contact
- security contact
- billing contact
- legal entity information
- effective Business Terms, DPA and AUP
- acceptance of required current versions
- active/trial/manual-contract billing
- active owner

Platform readiness must also be green before activation can be requested/approved.

## Operational automation

The existing `trustrelay-webhook-maintenance-v09` Cron job remains the single canonical webhook scheduler at `* * * * *`.

Release-gate verification after the v1.0 dispatcher change recorded 12 consecutive successful runs.

A daily retention **dry-run** job is implemented at `20 3 * * *`. Destructive retention remains disabled. The retention gate remains failed because the six retention policies still require approved retention periods/actions and legal basis.

## Production runtime

Sandbox organizations continue within sandbox plan limits.

Live organizations are checked on every new Partner API decision:

- production approval must remain approved
- billing must remain active/trialing/manual_contract
- plan decision quota must have capacity

A live organization whose billing or approval becomes invalid fails closed.
