# TrustRelay v1.0 Remaining Production Blockers

Updated: 2026-10-03 UTC

TrustRelay is a hardened production candidate. The technical platform is live and fail-closed for commercial activation. Production Checkout also fails closed with `COMMERCIAL_LAUNCH_NOT_READY` until every required platform control passes.

## Open required controls

### 1. custom_domain — FAIL
A production-owned custom domain must be purchased/controlled and attached before commercial launch. The managed Render hostname remains available for technical validation.

### 2. legal_entity — FAIL
The contracting legal entity, legal name, business address, and contracting authority must be confirmed. The owner intends to form an LLC later; TrustRelay must not invent or pre-date that entity.

### 3. legal_counsel_review — FAIL
Consumer Terms, Privacy Notice, Business Terms, DPA, AUP, retention terms, and launch language require qualified legal review. No automation may represent itself as counsel or mark this control passed.

### 4. legal_package_published — FAIL
Current legal records remain non-binding drafts. Effective/binding publication follows entity confirmation and qualified legal review.

### 5. retention_automation — FAIL
Engineering is complete: six policies are seeded, daily dry-runs are active, real candidate accounting is implemented, and destructive execution is disabled. Final retention periods/actions still require documented business/legal approval before approval or deletion automation is enabled.

- Manual identity review is disabled at the database boundary while TrustRelay is operated by one person. Re-enable only after two independent approved/trained/unexpired reviewers exist.
