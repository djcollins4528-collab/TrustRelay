## Deliberate production boundaries

v0.9 does not claim:

- SIEM certification
- SOC 2 compliance by virtue of export capability
- legal-record retention compliance for any specific jurisdiction
- guaranteed webhook delivery beyond the bounded retry/dead-letter model
- strict DNS-rebinding protection at infrastructure egress level

Those claims require deployment-specific controls and external compliance validation.
