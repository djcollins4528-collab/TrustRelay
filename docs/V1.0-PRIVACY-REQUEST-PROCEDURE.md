# TrustRelay v1.0 Privacy Request Procedure

Status: production-candidate process — requires monitored privacy contact and counsel-approved jurisdictional deadlines before commercial launch.

## Intake

Requests may include access, correction, deletion, restriction or other applicable privacy rights. Confirm the requester using the strongest reasonable account/identity evidence available before disclosing or deleting records.

## Search scope

Potential TrustRelay records include:
- account/person profile;
- grants, invitations and credentials;
- identity-proofing/evidence metadata and private documents;
- organization memberships;
- authorization decisions and evidence requests;
- notifications;
- compliance export metadata;
- billing metadata;
- security/audit records.

## Exceptions

Do not automatically erase records needed for fraud prevention, security, dispute preservation, accounting/tax, credential revocation evidence, legal obligations or valid institutional audit requirements. Those exceptions require legal review.

## Execution

1. Log the request and verified identity.
2. Determine TrustRelay/customer controller-processor roles.
3. Identify systems and records.
4. Export or correct applicable records.
5. For deletion, apply the approved retention policy and documented exceptions.
6. Record completion without placing sensitive request content into unnecessary operational logs.
7. Notify the requester through the approved channel.

## Enterprise requests

If TrustRelay processes data for an institution as processor/service provider, route the request to the institution when the agreement/law makes the institution responsible, while providing reasonable assistance.
