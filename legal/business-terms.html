Scope

These terms are intended for organizations using TrustRelay's Verifier Portal or Partner API to evaluate delegated authority. An order form or subscription checkout will identify the plan, commercial terms, contracting entity and effective date.

Customer responsibilities

  • Use the API only for legitimate authorization workflows.
  • Protect API keys and webhook secrets.
  • Assign least-privilege organization roles.
  • Independently determine legal sufficiency before relying on a credential.
  • Do not use TrustRelay as the sole basis for decisions prohibited by law or requiring independent review.

Data and security

The DPA and Security Statement form part of the intended enterprise package. Private identity evidence is not included in routine compliance exports and is accessible to institutions only through explicit evidence-sharing workflows.

Billing

Subscription billing is designed for Stripe-hosted Checkout and Billing Portal. Pricing and live payment configuration are not effective until configured in the production environment.
