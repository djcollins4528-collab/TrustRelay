Privacy Notice

DRAFT FOR LEGAL REVIEW — NOT YET EFFECTIVE. The commercial operator/legal entity and contact details must be confirmed before publication.

This notice describes how TrustRelay is designed to handle personal information when people create scoped authority grants, verify identity, act as representatives, or use institutional verification tools.

Information we process

  • Account and authentication information, including email address, display name, authentication/session metadata and account status.
  • Delegated-authority data, including principal/representative relationships, allowed actions, resources, limits, validity dates, revocation state and signed credential identifiers.
  • Identity-proofing information, including assurance level, proofing status, private identity or supporting documents, integrity hashes and reviewer decisions.
  • Institutional information, including organization profile, members/roles, API keys metadata, webhook configuration, decisions, evidence requests and compliance exports.
  • Operational/security information, including audit events, rate-limit records, delivery attempts, device/browser metadata supplied by infrastructure, and billing event identifiers.
  • Billing information when enabled. Payment card details are intended to be collected by Stripe-hosted payment surfaces rather than stored by TrustRelay.

Why we process it

  • Provide authentication, identity assurance and scoped delegation.
  • Issue, verify and revoke authority credentials.
  • Evaluate institutional authorization requests and preserve audit evidence.
  • Secure the service, prevent abuse, investigate incidents and maintain availability.
  • Administer organization access, support, billing and compliance exports.
  • Meet applicable legal obligations and enforce agreements.

Sharing

TrustRelay is designed to disclose data only as required to operate the service. Private evidence documents are not made broadly available to institutions; an institution receives access only after a user explicitly attaches a document to that institution's evidence request, subject to TrustRelay access controls.

Infrastructure subprocessors are listed on the Subprocessors page.

Security

Your choices

Users can control notification preferences and may request access, correction, deletion or restriction as applicable. Identity evidence may be subject to legal, fraud-prevention or dispute-preservation requirements.
