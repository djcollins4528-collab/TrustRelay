TRUST CENTER · v1.0

Security Statement

PRODUCTION-CANDIDATE STATEMENT. This page describes implemented controls but does not claim SOC 2, ISO 27001, PCI DSS, HIPAA or other certification.

Authentication

Passwordless email magic-link/OTP for users. Sensitive internal reviewer privileges are separately provisioned.

Authorization

Organization RBAC, scoped API keys, grant-specific policy evaluation and server-side privileged functions.

Private data

Identity evidence and compliance exports use private object storage and short-lived signed URLs.

Integrity

ES256 credentials, SHA-256 evidence/export hashes, and hash-chained organization audit events.

Webhooks

Vault-backed secrets, canonical JSON, HMAC-SHA256 signatures, retries, attempt logs and dead-letter state.

Database

Public-schema application tables use RLS with restrictive client-deny policies; privileged access is server-side.

Current production posture

  • Dedicated staging and production Supabase projects and dedicated Render services.
  • Passwordless email authentication with Turnstile CAPTCHA and configured Auth rate limits.
  • Custom SMTP, database SSL enforcement, and direct Postgres/pooler network restrictions enabled.
  • Nightly encrypted cross-project backups with a tested recovery rehearsal.
  • Fail-closed static malware scanning for the narrow PDF/JPEG/PNG/WebP evidence allowlist.
  • Monitored security, privacy, and incident contacts with an incident-response runbook.
  • Manual identity review disabled while TrustRelay is operated by one person.

Remaining commercial launch controls

  • Production-owned custom domain.
  • Confirmed contracting legal entity.
  • Qualified legal review and publication of the binding legal package.
  • Approved retention periods/actions before destructive retention is enabled.

Current non-claims

TrustRelay does not currently claim biometric identity matching, liveness detection, AML/sanctions screening, regulatory KYC certification, or independent security-framework certification.
