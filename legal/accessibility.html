ACCESSIBILITY

Accessibility Statement

TrustRelay is designed so principals, representatives, and institutional users can complete important authority workflows without unnecessary barriers. Accessibility is treated as an ongoing product requirement rather than a one-time claim.

Semantic structure

Public and authenticated interfaces use semantic HTML, labeled controls, headings, landmarks, and explicit button and form semantics where practical.

Keyboard interaction

Core workflows are designed for keyboard navigation without requiring pointer-only interaction.

Readable presentation

Interfaces use responsive layouts, visible text labels, high-contrast presentation, and avoid relying solely on color to communicate critical state.

Assistive technology feedback

Accessibility barriers can be reported through Support so they can be reproduced, prioritized, and remediated.

Current non-claim. TrustRelay has not completed an independent WCAG conformance audit and does not currently claim formal WCAG certification or legal accessibility compliance. The product is being developed toward broadly accepted accessible-web practices, and external validation can be added as the commercial platform matures.
