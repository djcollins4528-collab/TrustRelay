Data Processing Addendum

PRE-LAUNCH DRAFT — COUNSEL REVIEW REQUIRED. Roles, transfer mechanisms, governing law and annex details must be tailored to the contracting entity and customer jurisdiction.

Roles

For organization-controlled verification workflows, the customer is expected to act as controller/business and TrustRelay as processor/service provider for customer-submitted personal data, except where TrustRelay independently determines purposes required to secure, bill or operate the service.

Processing instructions

TrustRelay will process personal data to provide authentication, delegated-authority verification, identity/evidence workflows, audit records, support, security and billing, according to documented customer instructions and the applicable agreement.

Security measures

  • Private object storage for evidence and exports.
  • Short-lived signed download links.
  • RLS-denied public database tables with privileged server-side access paths.
  • Passwordless authentication and organization RBAC.
  • Hash-chained organization audit events and decision/evidence hashes.
  • Vault-backed webhook signing secrets and HMAC signatures.

Subprocessors

See the current Subprocessor List. Contractual flow-down terms and notice periods require counsel approval.

Data subject requests, incidents and deletion

TrustRelay will provide reasonable assistance using available records and controls. Required response timeframes, deletion exceptions and incident-notification commitments must be finalized in the effective DPA.

International transfers

Transfer mechanisms, SCC modules and regional hosting commitments are not represented as complete until counsel confirms the customer/processor roles and launch geography.
