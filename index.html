The product

Delegation without the ambiguity.

Traditional authority documents can be broad, static, difficult to validate, and disconnected from the transaction taking place. TrustRelay adds a digital control layer around delegated authority so every participant can understand the same boundaries.

01

Grant precise authority

A principal can define what a representative may do without handing over unlimited control.

  • Allowed and prohibited actions
  • Resource and transaction limits
  • Effective and expiration dates
  • Identity and evidence requirements
02

Carry verifiable proof

Accepted grants produce signed authority credentials that preserve the granted scope and assurance state.

  • ES256 signed credentials
  • Principal and representative binding
  • Assurance-at-issuance snapshot
  • Immediate revocation support
03

Verify before acting

Institutions can evaluate a requested action against the live grant instead of relying on a document alone.

  • ALLOW / DENY / ESCALATE decisions
  • Current revocation and expiration state
  • Evidence sharing by explicit consent
  • Auditable decision records

Security by design

Authority infrastructure should fail closed.

TrustRelay is engineered around least privilege, explicit authorization, short-lived internal capabilities, server-enforced role controls, private evidence boundaries, and auditable operations.

Passwordless account access

User-facing authentication avoids reusable account passwords.

Institutional MFA controls

Sensitive institutional operations are bound to higher-assurance sessions.

One-time internal capabilities

Internal privileged calls use short-lived, request-bound, single-consumption capabilities.

Private evidence storage

Evidence stays private and is shared with institutions through explicit workflow boundaries.

Server-enforced RBAC

Owner, admin, compliance, verifier, developer, and auditor permissions are enforced server-side.

Hash-linked audit evidence

Administrative activity and compliance exports include integrity controls for later review.

Signed webhook delivery

Webhook lifecycle controls include signing, retry history, dead-letter handling, and secret rotation.

Fail-closed production gates

Production activation and approved-code deployment are intentionally blocked when required controls are missing.
