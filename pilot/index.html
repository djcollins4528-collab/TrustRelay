Who this is for

The strongest pilots involve teams that repeatedly validate a person acting for another person, estate, trust or dependent.

  • Banks, credit unions, wealth firms and trust companies processing Financial POA, trustee or executor authority.
  • Estate, trust and fiduciary software platforms whose users must establish authority with outside institutions.
  • Caregiving platforms, health plans and benefits organizations whose members coordinate or act for loved ones.
  • Insurers and beneficiary-support platforms handling estate, claim and representative workflows.

Best first workflows

  • Financial Power of Attorney presented to a bank, advisor, insurer or benefits administrator.
  • Executor or personal representative contacting a financial institution during estate settlement.
  • Trustee, trust officer, protector or adviser proving role and scope to an outside institution.
  • Caregiver or family representative acting for an older adult or dependent.
  • Healthcare agent or authorized representative whose permission must be verified by a provider or plan.
