Best pilot workflows

  • Financial Power of Attorney presented to a bank, advisor, insurer, or benefits administrator.
  • Caregiver or family representative acting for an older adult or dependent.
  • Executor, trustee, guardian, conservator, or representative payee establishing authority with an outside organization.
  • Healthcare agent or authorized representative whose scope must be verified by a provider or plan.

What we measure

  • Time required to validate authority.
  • Repeated document requests and manual handoffs.
  • Ambiguous or incomplete permission cases.
  • Escalation volume.
  • Revocation visibility and audit completeness.

Pilot structure

One real workflow. One small internal team. No-cost for 60 days. Founder-led implementation support. We begin with the portal and only add API integration if the workflow proves value.

Security posture

TrustRelay uses passwordless authentication, CAPTCHA/rate limiting, private evidence storage, scoped RBAC, fail-closed evidence scanning, pinned-TLS webhook egress, encrypted tested disaster recovery, and production activation gates. Manual identity review is disabled while the service operates under the current solo-operator model.

Commercial status: design-partner pilots are available now. Paid production activation remains fail-closed until the final external legal/domain/retention launch controls are completed.
