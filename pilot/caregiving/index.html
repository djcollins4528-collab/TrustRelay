CAREGIVING · HEALTH PLANS · BENEFITS

Move a family caregiver from “helping” to verifiably authorized.

Caregiver platforms are excellent at navigation and support. The friction appears when the caregiver crosses into an outside organization that needs proof the person may actually speak, coordinate or act for the member.

Request a 20-minute pilot reviewEstimate the operational cost

Portable authority

A scoped grant can travel with the caregiver across organizations instead of requiring the family to re-explain the relationship every time.

Clear boundaries

Distinguish “may receive information,” “may coordinate,” and “may transact/consent” rather than treating all caregiver relationships alike.

Revocation visibility

The relying organization sees current status instead of relying solely on an old document or note.

Auditability

Record what was verified when a plan, provider or service partner relied on the representative.

Strong pilot use cases

  • Family caregiver coordinating benefits or services for an older adult.
  • Authorized representative interacting with a health plan or benefits administrator.
  • Caregiver navigating financial/legal tasks for a dependent family member.
  • Cross-organization handoffs where each party currently asks for separate proof.

Founding pilot

One member journey, one small team, 60 days, no cost. We measure verification time, repeated paperwork, escalations, ambiguous permission cases and handoffs.

Designed for a controlled evaluation: no production member data is required for the first workflow test. Paid commercial activation remains fail-closed until final external launch controls are completed.
