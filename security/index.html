SECURITY REPORTING

Vulnerability Disclosure

TrustRelay welcomes responsible reports that help us identify and remediate security weaknesses before they affect customers. Please report suspected vulnerabilities privately and do not post sensitive findings in public issues, forums, or social media.

Report a security issue security.txt Security Statement
Current reporting route. Until TrustRelay completes its custom-domain email cutover, security reports use the dedicated TrustRelay support mailbox with the subject SECURITY REPORT. A domain-based security mailbox will replace this route during production-domain activation.

What to include

Give us enough to reproduce the issue.

Clear, minimally invasive reproduction information lets us validate and remediate reports faster.

Affected surface

Identify the page, endpoint, workflow, account type, or integration involved.

Reproduction steps

Provide a concise sequence of actions, expected behavior, actual behavior, and any sanitized request or response details.

Security impact

Explain what an attacker could gain, alter, disclose, bypass, or disrupt.

Evidence

Include screenshots, logs, proof-of-concept details, or timestamps with secrets, personal data, and third-party data removed wherever possible.

Response process

What happens after a report.

TrustRelay triages reports according to exploitability, affected data or privileges, blast radius, and operational impact.

1. Intake

The report is reviewed privately and correlated with current logs, deployment state, and affected controls.

2. Validation

We reproduce the issue when possible and determine severity, scope, compensating controls, and whether immediate containment is required.

3. Remediation

Fixes move through the same security gates, static analysis, DAST, and approved-commit production controls used for normal releases.

4. Closure

Material incidents follow the documented incident-response process. Public disclosure timing is coordinated when disclosure is appropriate.

No bounty promise or certification claim. TrustRelay does not currently operate a paid bug-bounty program and does not represent this policy as an independent certification, legal safe-harbor agreement, or waiver of applicable law. The policy is intended to provide a clear private reporting channel and good-faith testing boundaries.
